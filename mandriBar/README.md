# mandribar

A progress bar above the prompt for every prompt, with an animated pixel Claude crab riding the fill.

- The bar moves on a log scale of time, calibrated by how long your own past prompts took, so long sessions keep moving.
- The crab holds a prop for what Claude is doing (magnifier, wrench, terminal, globe, pencil), raises a sign when Claude waits for your approval, and dozes when nothing happens.
- Each tool call leaves a coloured tick on the bar. A chime, a burst and a victory dance mark the finish; a sad crab marks a stop or an error.
- Fills (neon grid, smooth glow, candy stripes, liquid wave, matrix rain, segments), particle trails, presets, a colour palette, outfits that unlock with levels, a context meter and a chart of past prompts.
- With mandricrew installed, the crew's crabs show on Claude's working row above the bar.

## Use

`/mandribar`, or the ⚙ on the bar, opens the settings. ✕ hides the bar until the next prompt. Settings, level and history are per person and start fresh.
